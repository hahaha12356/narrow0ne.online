Narrow One Secret Fruits: Locations on Every Map

There is exactly one hidden fruit on every Narrow One map — a developer easter egg that gives no gameplay bonus and exists purely to be found. Fruits hide in secluded or hard-to-reach spots: above spawns, behind breakable-looking walls, under the map. Some need precise jumps, a few need specific speed gear, and the Gatehouse persimmon needs a key and an eyepatch. This page walks you to all of them.

Quick Answers: the Famous Three

  • Desert fruit → a cactus, hidden underneath the Desert map. Run behind a dune, sprint to the black line before it kills you, then clear the 3-1-2 door sequence and the maze beyond. It damages you if you touch it.
  • Wall fruit (Walls map) → a dragon fruit on the second floor of the 3rd building behind the enemy spawn. Jump through the window, climb the words carved on the wall, and it's on a table.
  • Castle fruit → a strawberry in a backroom above your spawn. Get on the spawn roof, find the window with the grey roof, slide down the vent — it's on a plate.

Desert Fruit: Full Route

The Desert cactus lives underground. From spawn, run behind a dune and rotate your camera until the flag icon sits above the dune — that lines up the sprint to the black line. Cross it fast or the map kills you. Once under, take the doors in the order 3, 1, 2, then navigate the maze and parkour sections. The cactus itself deals damage on contact, so don't hug it for the screenshot.

Walls Fruit (Dragon Fruit): Full Route

Cross the Great Wall toward the enemy side and count three buildings past their spawn. Jump through the window of the third one, then climb the words carved onto the wall like footholds. On the second floor the dragon fruit waits on a table — one of the few fruits you can grab without parkour skills, if you survive the enemy territory walk in.

Other Easter Eggs Worth Knowing

  • Ringing bells: shoot the church bell on Alleys or the bell on Fields — the whole map hears it.
  • Mt. Fuji: visible in the Towers skybox — a nod to the Arch map's Japan theme.
  • The hot dog on Halls honors "hot dog man", an early Narrow One community personality.
  • Ducks on Ruins reference the developers' other game, Ducklings.io.
  • Gatehouse chessboard: the Guard Key door leads past a sniping gate and a chess-pattern floor with invisible walls — walk the pattern, don't sprint it.

Before You Hunt: Gear Checks

Most fruit routes are jump puzzles; a few are gated. Bring near-max movement speed (the gear guide lists the fastest pieces) — Gatehouse explicitly requires around 20/20 speed. Buy the Guard Key (2,000 coins, melee shop section) and equip the sixth eye or the eyepatch from Looks. Fruits give zero stat rewards, so treat the hunt as a badge run: the community "found them all" flex since 2021.
